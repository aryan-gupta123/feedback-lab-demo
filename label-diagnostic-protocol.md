# Post-hoc label diagnostic: reliability-labels-v1

This is a separate exploratory diagnostic motivated after reviewing the completed 48-path primary study. Qwen3 selected asset_b on 47/48 inverted-source questions. The primary audit found matching raw/scored choices and no context truncation (maximum 775 prompt tokens under context 4096). We do not change, rerun or pool the primary data, models, prompts, scoring or hypotheses.

Freeze before diagnostic inference: four new seeds 62300–62303, public_only and inverted conditions, each presented in original and renamed forms to both previously eligible checkpoints. Total: 4 paths × 2 conditions × 2 naming variants × 2 checkpoints = 32 new local requests. No paid API, retries, response repair, favorable-direction gate, additional seeds, or early-stopping for favorable results. On an invalid response stop and preserve incomplete evidence. Main study remains 443 attempts; this diagnostic adds at most 32, for 475 combined across these two phases.

The same simulator and already frozen prompt/choice-only schema/CPU settings are reused. Rename asset_a ↔ asset_b throughout each user input, including current public/forecast keys, historical forecast/settlement keys and available-option identifiers. Preserve field insertion order and numerical values, so physical information and its sequence stay unchanged while literal asset names are exchanged. Cash remains cash. The output schema still permits the same three literal labels. Score renamed choices against correspondingly permuted evaluator values; map each response back to physical assets for comparison. Applying the rename twice must reproduce the exact original user serialization.

Endpoint: descriptive physical-action agreement for each original/renamed pair, reported separately for public_only and inverted (four paired contexts per checkpoint/condition), plus literal choice frequencies and every raw input/reply. There are only four independent paths: no p-values, confidence intervals, model-family generalization, or integration into the original primary estimands. This diagnostic can expose dependence on arbitrary asset naming/presentation. It does not isolate name preference from schema/order effects, identify internal reasoning or prove a psychological trust mechanism. Do not retune the prompt until the diagnostic appears favorable.
